Understanding Refunds

Behind every refund request there's a child who was building something and stopped — and you are almost always the first person who could have noticed it. This training is about how well you observe, and how fast you act.

👀Learn exactly what to watch for, during the lesson and between lessons.
🖥️Practice reading a group's Progress view in a hands-on BO simulation.
🧭Know exactly what's yours to fix, what's yours to report, and who to tell.

Work through 7 modules and a final checklist, then head to the DST. Each module needs 70%+ to unlock the next.
